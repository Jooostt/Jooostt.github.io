About Idle Butterfly Garden

Adopt butterflies, fill your flower beds and watch the nectar roll in, even while your phone is in your pocket. Collect 18 different butterflies, grow them from cocoons, upgrade your garden and move to bigger habitats. The whole game works offline.

Free to play. The app shows ads, which you can remove with one optional purchase; no account required.
