Mahjong solitaire with big, clear tiles. No clock, unlimited hints and undo, and it works offline.

Coming soon to Google Play

About Willow Mahjong

Match pairs of free tiles until the board is empty. The tiles are big and easy to read, free tiles stand out with a dark border, and there is no clock unless you want one. Hints, undo and shuffle are unlimited and cost nothing; a shuffle always leaves a board you can finish. Choose from 44 boards, from small to large, or play the daily board that is the same for everyone and keep your streak going. A high-contrast and a colour-blind-friendly mode are built in, and everything works offline.

Free to play. The app shows ads, and an optional one-time purchase removes the banner and full-screen ads; no account required.
