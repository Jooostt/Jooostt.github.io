About 2048 Merge

Play 2048 Merge, the classic number puzzle — anytime, anywhere, fully offline. Swipe the board, merge equal tiles and build your way to 2048 and beyond!

🔢How to play

Swipe up, down, left or right to slide all tiles. Two equal tiles that collide merge into one tile of double the value: 2 + 2 = 4, 4 + 4 = 8 … all the way to 2048 and beyond. A new tile appears after every move; when the board is full and nothing can merge, the game is over.

🎯Four difficulty levels

  • Easy: roomy 6×6 board to 2048
  • Medium: 5×5 to 2048
  • Hard: the classic 4×4 to 2048
  • Expert: 4×4 to 4096 — for true strategists

🗺️Adventure mode

Play through hundreds of numbered levels that keep getting harder: the goal grows, the board shrinks. Unlock them one by one, earn a medal on each and grab milestone rewards along the way.

📅Daily challenge

One unique board every day, the same for everyone. Earn a medal from 1 to 5 stars and build a streak: 3, 7, 30 or even 100 days in a row!

🧰Boosts when you're stuck

Boxed in? Clear all 2s and 4s with the clean-up boost, or take a second chance after game over that keeps your highest tiles.

🪙Coins and themes

Earn coins with every goal you reach and spend them on boosts or new looks: Forest, Space, Neon, Wood, Marble, Christmas and Halloween. Cosmetic only — no pay-to-win.

🏅And more

  • 27 achievements, from your first goal to a hundred-day streak
  • Detailed statistics: best score, highest tile, perfect games and more
  • Zen mode: turn off the timer and play relaxed
  • Sound and haptic feedback (can be turned off in settings)
  • Available in English, Dutch, Spanish, German, French and Portuguese

Free to play. The app shows ads; no purchases or accounts required.
