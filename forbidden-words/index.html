Forbidden Words

Explain the word without saying it! A fast and funny party word game for friends and family.

About Forbidden Words

Forbidden Words is the fast and funny party game where you explain a word to your team without saying the five forbidden words on the card. Pick your teams, pass the phone around and see who keeps a straight face under pressure.

🗣️How it works

Each turn, one player sees a card with a word and five forbidden words. They have 60 seconds to get their team to guess as many words as possible. Say a forbidden word and a player from the other team presses Forbidden: it costs a point and the card does not count.

🎉Why you'll love it

  • Quick to learn, hard to put down
  • Perfect for parties, road trips, family nights and ice-breakers
  • Great for 4 to 20+ players
  • Over 1,000 cards, from food and animals to movies, music and sayings
  • Review every card after the turn and settle any disagreement

⚙️Play your way

  • Unlimited teams, as many as you like
  • Custom target score for a quick game or an epic battle
  • Make your own cards with up to five forbidden words
  • Clean, modern interface with a calm mint design

🌍Dutch and English

  • Every language has its own cards, not translations of one list
  • The full interface is translated too
  • Switch language straight from the setup screen

🎯Tips

  • No gestures, sound effects, first letters or rhymes
  • Skip a tricky card and move on; speed beats perfection
  • Let a player from the other team guard the card
