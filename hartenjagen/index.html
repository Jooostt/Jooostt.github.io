Hearts

The classic Hearts card game. Play offline against clever computer opponents.

About Hearts

Play Hearts, the classic card game — anytime, anywhere, fully offline. It's you against three computer players: who collects the fewest penalty points?

♥How to play

Every heart in your tricks costs 1 point, and the queen of spades a painful 13. Follow suit, dodge the points — or go all-in and shoot the moon by taking all 26 penalty points in a single round, handing them to your opponents instead. The game ends when someone reaches the target score; the player with the fewest points wins.

New to the rules? Built-in tips explain the game step by step while you play.

♠Clever opponents

Three difficulty levels, from friendly to fierce. On Hard, the computer counts cards, builds voids and even notices when you are quietly trying to shoot the moon.

♦Play by your rules

  • Adjustable target score: 50, 75 or 100 points
  • Passing: rotating, always left, always right or never
  • Variants: jack of diamonds is −10, queen of spades breaks hearts
  • Moon scoring: +26 for the rest or −26 for the shooter

♣And more

  • Earn 18 achievements, from your first victory to a flawless game
  • Detailed statistics: win streaks, average score and more
  • Pause your game and pick it up later, right where you left off
  • Sound and haptic feedback (can be turned off in settings)
  • Works in landscape and on tablets

Free to play. The app shows ads; no purchases or accounts required.
