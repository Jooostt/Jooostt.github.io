About Pipe Connect

Play Pipe Connect, the classic pipe puzzle — anytime, anywhere, fully offline. Drag pipes between the colored endpoints and fill the whole board!

🔧How to play

Every level shows a grid with pairs of colored endpoints. Drag your finger from an endpoint to lay a pipe to the matching one. Pipes cannot cross, and the level is only solved when all pairs are connected and every cell is filled. Drag over an existing pipe and it gets cut — so you can always rearrange.

🎯Four difficulty levels

  • Easy: 5×5 with 4–6 pairs
  • Medium: 6×6 with 5–7 pairs
  • Hard: 7×7 with 6–9 pairs
  • Expert: 9×9 with up to 12 colors — for the true master plumber

🗺️Adventure mode

Travel as a plumber through eight pipe worlds: Waterworks, Swamp, Factory, Desert, Deep sea, Volcano, Ice cave and Space. Endless numbered levels that gradually get harder, with a medal per level, milestone bonuses and gates to the next world.

📅Daily puzzle

One unique 8×8 puzzle every day, the same for everyone. Earn a medal from 1 to 5 stars and build a streak: 3, 7, 30 or even 100 days in a row!

🪙Coins and themes

Earn coins with every solved level and spend them on hints or new looks: Pastel, Dark, Light, Spring, Summer, Forest, Space, Neon, Wood, Marble, Christmas and Halloween — plus confetti styles. Cosmetic only — no pay-to-win.

🏅And more

  • Hints via coins or a short ad
  • Stuck? Have one complete connection placed for you
  • 23 achievements, from your first level to a hundred days in a row
  • Detailed statistics: fastest time, perfect levels and more
  • Zen mode: turn off the timer and puzzle relaxed
  • Sound and haptic feedback (can be turned off in the settings)
  • Available in English, Dutch, Spanish, German, French and Portuguese

Free to play. The app shows ads; no purchases or accounts required.
