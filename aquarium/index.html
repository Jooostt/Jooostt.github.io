About Idle Aquarium

Buy fish, fill your tank and watch the coins roll in, even while your phone is in your pocket. Collect 18 different fish, hatch mystery eggs, upgrade your tank and move to bigger rooms. The whole game works offline.

Free to play. The app shows ads, which you can remove with one optional purchase; no account required.
