Minesweeper

The classic minesweeper. Endless minefields and a fresh daily challenge.

About Minesweeper

Play Minesweeper, the classic brain teaser — anytime, anywhere, fully offline. Open every safe cell without hitting a mine. The numbers tell you how many mines hide in the neighbouring cells!

💣How to play

Every minefield is freshly generated and your first tap is always safe. Tap to open a cell, long-press to place a flag. Hit a mine? That costs a life; three mines end the game.

🎯Four difficulty levels

  • Easy: a small board with few mines, perfect for a quick break
  • Medium: the classic minesweeper
  • Hard: a bigger board with more mines
  • Expert: for the true sweeper

🗺️Adventure mode

Play through hundreds of numbered levels that get progressively harder. Travel through colourful worlds, earn a medal on every level and grab milestone rewards along the way.

📅Daily puzzle

One unique minefield every day. Earn a medal of 1 to 5 stars and build a streak: 3, 7, 30 or even 100 days in a row!

🪙Coins and themes

Earn coins with every completed game and spend them on hints or new looks: Field, Night, Classic, Desert, Ocean, Camo, Neon, Marble, Spring, Summer, Christmas and Halloween. Cosmetic only — no pay-to-win.

🏅And more

  • Help with coins
  • Mine scanner that flags three mines for you
  • 23 achievements, from your first board to a hundred-day streak
  • Detailed statistics: fastest time, flawless games and more
  • Zen mode: no lives, just relaxed sweeping
  • Sound and haptic feedback (can be turned off in settings)
  • Available in English, Dutch, Spanish, German, French and Portuguese

Free to play. The app shows ads; no purchases or accounts required.
