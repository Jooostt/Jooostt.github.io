Word Connect

Connect letters into words. Endless puzzles and a fresh daily challenge.

About Word Connect

Play Word Connect, the addictive word puzzle — anytime, anywhere, fully offline. Swipe across the letters on the wheel and fill the crossword grid!

🔗How to play

Every level hands you a wheel of letters. Connect the letters with a swipe to build words; every word you find fills its spot in the crossword grid. The level is complete once the grid is full.

⭐Bonus words

Build a valid word that is not in the grid? You earn extra coins right away. The bigger your vocabulary, the richer you get!

🎯Four difficulty levels

  • Easy: 5 letters, 4 words
  • Medium: 6 letters, 6 words
  • Hard: 7 letters, 8 words
  • Expert: 7 letters, 10 words — for true wordsmiths

🗺️Adventure mode

Play through hundreds of numbered levels that get gradually harder. Unlock them one by one, earn a medal on each and travel through ever-changing worlds: from meadow and beach to jungle and space.

📅Daily puzzle

One unique puzzle every day, the same for everyone. Earn a medal from 1 to 5 stars and build a streak: 3, 7, 30 or even 100 days in a row!

🪙Coins and themes

Earn coins with every completed level and spend them on hints or new looks: Forest, Space, Neon, Wood, Marble, Christmas and Halloween. Cosmetic only — no pay-to-win.

🏅And more

  • Hints with coins
  • 23 achievements, from your first level to a hundred-day streak
  • Detailed statistics: fastest time, perfect levels and more
  • Zen mode: hide the timer and puzzle at your own pace
  • Sound and haptic feedback (can be turned off in settings)
  • Available in English, Dutch, Spanish, German, French and Portuguese — the language also picks the word lists

Free to play. The app shows ads; no purchases or accounts required.
