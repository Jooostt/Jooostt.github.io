Coming soon to Google Play

About Clearview Solitaire

Clearview Solitaire is made for easy reading: big cards with large numbers, a calm table colour of your choice and a high-contrast option. Play Classic Solitaire (Klondike), FreeCell or Spider. Undo and hints have no limit, there is no time pressure, and everything works offline. Classic Solitaire and FreeCell deals are checked so that they can be won. A new daily deal is waiting every day, the same for everyone, and your streak keeps count.

Free to play. The app shows ads, and an optional one-time purchase removes the banner and full-screen ads; no account required.
