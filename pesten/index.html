About Pesten

Pesten is the classic Dutch card game, related to Crazy Eights and Mau-Mau. Play a card of the same suit or value and be the first to get rid of all your cards. Play against the computer on three levels or with friends on one phone, and set your own house rules: 2 and joker stacking, 7 plays again, 8 skips, jack chooses the suit, ace reverses. The whole game works offline.

Het Nederlandse kaartspel Pesten, offline te spelen tegen de computer of met vrienden op één telefoon, met instelbare huisregels.

Free to play. The app shows ads, which you can remove with one optional purchase; no account required.
