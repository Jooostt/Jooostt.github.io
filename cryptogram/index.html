About Cryptogram

Play Cryptogram, the clever number code puzzle, fully offline. Every number stands for exactly one letter: decode them all in the daily challenge, a fresh set of cryptogram puzzles every day.

🔢How to play

Every puzzle is freshly generated: six to ten words around one theme, written in number code. Same letter = same number, everywhere in the puzzle. A few letters are given at the start. Tap a box, pick a letter on the letter bar and it is instantly filled in on every box with that number. Letters already in use turn grey — pure logical deduction.

🎯Four difficulty levels

  • Easy: 6 short words and 2 given letters
  • Medium: 7 words and 2 given letters
  • Hard: 8 words and 1 given letter
  • Expert: 10 words and 1 given letter — for the true codebreaker

🗺️Adventure mode

Travel through themed worlds of ten levels each, from easy to Expert. Unlock them one by one, earn a 1-to-5-star medal on every level and grab bonus coins at the milestones on level 10, 25, 50 and 100.

🐶Themes

Animals, food, sports, cars, countries, movies, music, beer, flowers, games, jobs, fish and travel. All words in a puzzle belong to the same theme — a helpful head start.

📅Daily puzzle

One unique puzzle a day, the same for everyone. Earn a 1-to-5-star medal and build a streak: 3, 7, 30 or even 100 days in a row!

💡Help when you are stuck

Reveal a letter or have your entries checked — wrong letters are marked in red. Even an Expert puzzle always stays solvable.

🪙Coins and themes

Earn coins with every completed level and spend them on new looks: Forest, Space, Neon, Wood, Marble and the seasonal themes Spring, Summer, Christmas and Halloween — plus confetti styles for your victory. Cosmetic only — no pay-to-win.

🏅And more

  • 23 achievements, from your first level to a hundred-day streak
  • Detailed statistics: fastest time, perfect levels and more
  • Zen mode: turn off the timer and puzzle at your own pace
  • Sound and haptic feedback (can be turned off in the settings)
  • Available in English, Dutch, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Turkish and Italian — the language also picks the word lists

Free to play. The app shows ads, which you can remove with one optional purchase; no account required.
