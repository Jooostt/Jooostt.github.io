About Country Quiz

Play Country Quiz and test your knowledge of the whole world — offline, anytime, anywhere. 193 countries (every UN member state), five kinds of question and ten questions per round.

🌍Five question types

  • Which country does this flag belong to?
  • What is the capital of this country?
  • Which country is this the capital of?
  • Which country has more inhabitants?
  • On which continent is this country?

The chips on the menu let you pick exactly what you want to practise: flags, capitals, population, continents — or all of them mixed.

🎯Four difficulty levels

  • Easy: world-famous countries only
  • Medium: one step further into the world
  • Hard: the less familiar countries too
  • Expert: everything, with distractors from the same region — neighbouring countries that really catch you out

The higher the level, the more coins every correct answer earns.

📅Daily quiz

Ten questions a day, the same for everyone in the world. Earn a 1-to-3-star medal and build your streak: 3, 7, 30 or even 100 days in a row!

🗺️World trip

Travel through worlds of ten levels each: first Europe, Asia, Africa, North America, South America and Oceania, then mixed worlds spanning the globe. Inside every world the difficulty rises from Easy to Expert, with a treasure chest on level 10 and bonus coins at the milestones on level 10, 25, 50 and 100.

💡Help when you hesitate

Use the 50/50 to strike out two wrong answers — it costs coins or a short ad. Missed anyway? Take a second chance and try the same question again.

🪙Coins and themes

Earn coins for every correct answer, plus a bonus for a flawless round. Spend them in the shop on twelve themes: Pastel, Dark, Light, Forest, Space, Neon, Wood, Marble and the seasonal themes Spring, Summer, Christmas and Halloween — plus confetti styles for your victory. Cosmetic only — no pay-to-win.

📊And more

  • 27 achievements, from your first round to a hundred-day streak
  • Statistics with your accuracy per question type and per difficulty
  • Country names and capitals in English, Dutch, Spanish, German, French and Portuguese
  • Sound and haptic feedback, both switchable in the settings
  • Optional reminder for the daily quiz
  • Fully offline: no internet needed to play, no account

Free to play. The app shows ads, which you can remove with one optional purchase; no account required.

Frequently asked questions

Yes. Country Quiz works without an internet connection, so you can play on a plane, on the train or anywhere without Wi-Fi.
