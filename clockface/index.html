About Clockface

Plenty of people never learned to read an analog clock properly, or have forgotten how. Clockface teaches it step by step: a short course from the two hands to exact minutes, then practice at five levels, from whole hours to single minutes. Drag the hands to set a time, read a clock and pick the right time, or match a time with the words, such as “quarter past three” (English and Dutch). Speed rounds build a streak, the quiz mixes everything, and five clock styles (classic, no numbers, watch, Roman numerals and a 24-hour dial) make it harder when you are ready. Your progress stays on your device, and no account is needed.

Free to use. The app shows ads; an optional one-time purchase removes banners and full-screen ads, and optional videos stay available.
