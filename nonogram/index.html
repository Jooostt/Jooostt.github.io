About Nonogram

Play Nonogram offline, the Japanese picture logic puzzle also known as griddlers. The numbers beside each row and above each column show the runs of filled squares: use logic to solve the puzzle and reveal the picture!

🧩How to play

Every puzzle is freshly generated and guaranteed to have exactly one solution, reachable by pure logic — you never have to guess. Pick fill ■ or cross ✕ and tap a square, or drag to do a whole run in one swipe. Filling a square that should stay empty is a mistake; 3 to 5 mistakes (depending on the grid size) end the game.

🎯Four difficulty levels

  • Easy: 5×5, perfect for a quick break
  • Medium: 10×10, the classic newspaper puzzle
  • Hard: 12×12, for the practised solver
  • Expert: 15×15 — for the true puzzler

🗺️Adventure mode

Play through hundreds of numbered levels that get progressively harder. Travel through colourful worlds, earn a medal on every level and grab milestone rewards along the way.

📅Daily puzzle

One unique nonogram every day, the same for everyone. Earn a medal of 1 to 5 stars and build a streak: 3, 7, 30 or even 100 days in a row!

🪙Coins and themes

Earn coins with every completed puzzle and spend them on hints or new looks: Forest, Space, Neon, Wood, Marble, Christmas and Halloween. Cosmetic only — no pay-to-win.

🏅And more

  • Hints for coins: the next correct square is filled in for you
  • Clues cross themselves off as soon as a line is right, and a finished line is crossed out for you
  • 23 achievements, from your first puzzle to a hundred-day streak
  • Detailed statistics: fastest time, flawless puzzles and more
  • Zen mode: hide the timer and solve at your own pace
  • Sound and haptic feedback (can be turned off in settings)
  • Available in English, Dutch, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Italian, Turkish, Polish, Romanian and Russian

Free to play. The app shows ads, which you can remove with one optional purchase; no account required.
