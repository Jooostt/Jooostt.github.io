Word Search

The classic word search. Endless puzzles and a fresh daily challenge.

About Word Search

Play Word Search, the classic puzzle — anytime, anywhere, fully offline. Drag across the letters and find all the hidden words!

🔍How to play

Every puzzle is freshly generated: a grid full of letters with themed words hidden inside. Drag your finger across the letters to select a word; found words stay highlighted. The level is complete once you have found them all.

🎯Four difficulty levels

  • Easy: 8×8, words across and down
  • Medium: 10×10, diagonals too
  • Hard: 14×14, backwards as well
  • Expert: 18×18, every direction — for true word detectives

🗺️Adventure mode

Play through hundreds of numbered levels that get gradually harder. Unlock them one by one, earn a medal on each and hit milestone rewards along the way.

🐶Themes

Animals, food, sports, cars, countries, movies, music, beer, flowers, games, jobs, fish and travel. Every level feels different.

📅Daily puzzle

One unique puzzle every day, the same for everyone. Earn a medal from 1 to 5 stars and build a streak: 3, 7, 30 or even 100 days in a row!

🪙Coins and themes

Earn coins with every completed level and spend them on hints or new looks: Forest, Space, Neon, Wood, Marble, Christmas and Halloween. Cosmetic only — no pay-to-win.

🏅And more

  • Hints with coins
  • 23 achievements, from your first level to a hundred-day streak
  • Detailed statistics: fastest time, perfect levels and more
  • Zen mode: hide the timer and puzzle at your own pace
  • Sound and haptic feedback (can be turned off in settings)
  • Available in English, Dutch, Spanish, German, French and Portuguese — the language also picks the word lists

Free to play. The app shows ads; no purchases or accounts required.
