About 30 Seconds

Play 30 Seconds, the fast and funny word-guessing party game that turns any get-together into a lively contest. Grab your friends, family or colleagues, pick your teams and get ready to laugh, shout and beat the clock.

⏱️How it works

One player gets 5 secret words and just 30 seconds to describe them to their team — without saying the word itself or a translation. Every word your team guesses scores a point. The first team to reach the target score wins!

🎉Why you'll love it

  • Quick to learn, impossible to put down
  • Perfect for parties, road trips, family nights and ice-breakers
  • Great for 4 to 20+ players
  • Rounds last only 30 seconds — no waiting around
  • A huge built-in word library, reshuffled every round

⚙️Play your way

  • Unlimited teams — add as many as you like
  • Custom target score: a quick game to 15 or an epic battle to 50
  • Clean, modern interface with a calm mint design
  • Built-in instructions so new players learn in seconds

🌍9 languages included

  • Dutch, English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Polish and Turkish
  • Every language has its own word list of thousands of words — not translations of one list
  • The full interface is translated too
  • Switch language straight from the setup screen, so a mixed group plays in whatever suits them
  • Add your own custom words per language

🎯Tips

  • Use synonyms, descriptions and examples — never the word itself
  • Skip a tough word and move on; speed beats perfection
  • Swap who describes each round so everyone gets a turn

Frequently asked questions

Is 30 Seconds free?

Yes. 30 Seconds is free to download and play. The app shows ads, which you can remove with one optional in-app purchase.

Does 30 Seconds work offline?

Yes. 30 Seconds works without an internet connection, so you can play on a plane, on the train or anywhere without Wi-Fi.

Do I need an account to play 30 Seconds?

No. There is no sign-up or login: open the app and play. Your games and settings stay on your own device.

How do you play 30 Seconds?

One player gets 5 secret words and just 30 seconds to describe them to their team — without saying the word itself or a translation. Every word your team guesses scores a point. The first team to reach the target score wins!
