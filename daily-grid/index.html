Daily Grid

Two daily grid puzzles, crowns and trails, that work offline. Solve them, keep your streak and practise at your own level.

Coming soon to Google Play

About Daily Grid

Every day brings two new puzzles, the same for everyone. In Crowns you place one crown in every row, column and coloured region without letting two crowns touch. In Trails you draw one line through every square, passing the numbered dots in order. Each puzzle has exactly one solution, and everything works offline. Keep your daily streak going, check your statistics, or practise at four difficulty levels.

Free to play. The app shows ads, and an optional one-time purchase removes the banner and full-screen ads; no account required.
