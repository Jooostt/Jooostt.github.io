Crossword

The classic crossword. Endless puzzles and a fresh daily challenge.

About Crossword

Play Crossword, the classic puzzle — anytime, anywhere, fully offline. Read the clues and fill in the grid!

✏️How to play

Every puzzle is freshly generated: a grid where all words cross each other, each with its own clue. Tap a square to pick a word (tap again to switch between across and down) and type with the keyboard. Correctly filled words turn green. The level is complete once the whole grid is right.

🎯Four difficulty levels

  • Easy: 7×7 with 6 words
  • Medium: 9×9 with 9 words
  • Hard: 11×11 with 13 words
  • Expert: 13×13 with 17 words — for true puzzlers

🗺️Adventure mode

Work your way through hundreds of numbered levels that get progressively harder. Unlock them one by one, earn a medal on each and grab milestone rewards along the way — through colourful worlds from forest to outer space.

📅Daily puzzle

One unique puzzle every day, the same for everyone. Earn a 1-to-5-star medal and build a streak: 3, 7, 30 or even 100 days in a row!

🪙Coins and themes

Earn coins with every completed level and spend them on hints or new looks: Forest, Space, Neon, Wood, Marble, Christmas and Halloween. Cosmetic only — no pay-to-win.

🏅And more

  • Hints reveal a letter or a whole word
  • 23 achievements, from your first level to a hundred-day streak
  • Detailed statistics: fastest time, perfect levels and more
  • Zen mode: hide the timer and puzzle at your own pace
  • Sound and haptic feedback (can be turned off in settings)
  • Available in English, Dutch, Spanish, German, French and Portuguese — the language also sets the clues

Free to play. The app shows ads; no purchases or accounts required.
