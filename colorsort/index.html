About Color Sort

Play Color Sort, the classic sorting puzzle — anytime, anywhere, fully offline. Pour the colors until every tube holds just one color!

🧪How to play

Tap a tube to pick it up, then tap a second tube to pour. You can only pour onto the same color or into an empty tube, and only when the whole top block fits. Every puzzle is freshly generated and always solvable.

🎯Four difficulty levels

  • Easy: 5 colors and 2 empty tubes — plenty of room to maneuver
  • Medium: 6 colors
  • Hard: 8 colors
  • Expert: 9 colors and just 1 empty tube — every move counts

🗺️Adventure mode

Travel the world map through endless numbered levels that get gradually harder. Every world of ten levels has its own vibe — from the ocean and the forest to candy, lava, ice, neon, the desert and outer space — and milestone levels hold a bonus reward.

📅Daily puzzle

One unique puzzle every day, the same for everyone. Earn a medal from 1 to 5 stars and build a streak: 3, 7, 30 or even 100 days in a row!

🪙Coins and themes

Earn coins with every completed level and spend them on extra undos or new looks: Forest, Space, Neon, Wood, Marble and seasonal themes like Spring, Summer, Christmas and Halloween — plus confetti styles for the celebration. Cosmetic only — no pay-to-win.

🏅And more

  • When you are stuck, a short ad earns you an extra tube or a shuffle
  • 23 achievements, from your first level to a hundred-day streak
  • Detailed statistics: fastest time, perfect levels and more
  • Zen mode: hide the timer and puzzle at your own pace
  • Sound and haptic feedback (can be turned off in settings)
  • Available in English, Dutch, Spanish, German, French and Portuguese

Free to play. The app shows ads, which you can remove with one optional purchase; no account required.
