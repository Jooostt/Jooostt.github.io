Number Match

About Number Match

Play Number Match: keep tapping two numbers that add up to 10 or are equal, and clear the board as you go. Learned in a minute, wonderfully hard to put down — anytime, anywhere, fully offline.

🔢How to play

Tap a number, then tap its partner: together they make 10 (like 3 and 7) or they are the same digit. The two must be able to see each other: right next to each other (diagonals count), or on the same row, column or diagonal with only empty cells in between. After every match everything drops down and new digits fall in from the top — the board keeps moving.

🎯Four difficulty levels

  • Easy: 6×6, a generous move budget to learn the ropes
  • Medium: 7×7, nicely balanced
  • Hard: 8×8, the board stays full — adjacency is king
  • Expert: 9×9, slim margins, for true number crunchers

🗺️Adventure

Travel through eight number worlds — Neon, Cosmos, Ocean, Jungle, Volcano, Ice, Desert and Aurora — with ten levels per world and milestone bonuses along the way. Every world has its own atmosphere on the map.

📅Daily puzzle

One unique board every day, the same for everyone. Earn a medal and build a streak: 3, 7, 30 or even 100 days in a row!

💡Help when you're stuck

Hints, a shuffle or +5 moves after a failed level are available for coins or a short ad — and if there is truly no move left, the board shuffles for free.

🪙Coins and themes

Earn coins with every completed level, the daily puzzle and the daily chest. Spend them in the shop on new looks: Light, Night, Pastel, Dark, Forest, Space, Neon, Wood, Marble and seasonal themes, plus confetti. Cosmetic only — no pay-to-win.

🏅And more

  • 24 achievements, from your first level to a hundred-day streak
  • Detailed statistics: fastest time, perfect levels and more
  • Zen mode: hide the timer and puzzle at your own pace
  • Sound and haptic feedback (can be turned off in settings)
  • Available in English, Dutch, Spanish, German, French and Portuguese

Free to play. The app shows ads; no purchases or accounts required.
