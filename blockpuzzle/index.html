Block Puzzle

Fill the board exactly. Always solvable, a fresh challenge every day.

About Block Puzzle

Play Block Puzzle, the calm fitting puzzle where you fill the board exactly — anytime, anywhere, fully offline. Drag the blocks into place until not a single square is left!

🧩How to play

Every board is freshly generated and carved into pieces. Drag a piece from the tray onto the board; the shadow shows where it will land. Green means it fits, red means it doesn't. The level is done the moment the whole board is filled.

✅Always solvable

The pieces come from one complete carving of the board, so there is always a solution — no dead ends. No rotating or flipping needed: each piece fits just as it sits in the tray. Placed one wrong? Tap it to take it back.

🎯Four difficulties

  • Easy: 5×5, a handful of pieces to ease you in
  • Medium: 6×6, proper puzzling
  • Hard: 7×7, more pieces and more choices
  • Expert: 8×8, up to twenty pieces — for real thinkers

🗺️Adventure mode

Work your way through hundreds of numbered levels that get steadily harder. Unlock them one by one, earn a medal on each and collect milestone rewards along the way.

🎨Twelve piece sets

Classic, Mini, Large, Snakes, Blocks, Towers, Mosaic, Crystal, Labyrinth, Confetti, Clouds and Lightning. Each set carves the board differently: winding snakes are tricky to place, chunky blocks are more forgiving. It makes every level feel different.

📅Daily puzzle

One unique puzzle a day, the same for everyone. Earn a medal from 1 to 5 stars and build a streak: 3, 7, 30 or even 100 days in a row!

🪙Coins and themes

Earn coins with every completed level and spend them on hints or new looks: Dark, Light, Forest, Space, Neon, Wood, Marble, Christmas and Halloween. Cosmetic only — no pay-to-win.

🏅And more

  • Hints with coins
  • 24 achievements, from your first level to a hundred days in a row
  • Detailed statistics: fastest time, perfect levels and more
  • Zen mode: turn the timer off and puzzle at your own pace
  • Landing shadow can be switched off for an extra challenge
  • Sound and haptic feedback (both optional in settings)
  • Available in English, Dutch, Spanish, German, French and Portuguese

Free to play. The app shows ads; no purchases or accounts needed.
