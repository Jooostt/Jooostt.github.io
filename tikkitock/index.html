Coming soon to Google Play

About Tikki Tock

Tikki is a little owl who looks after the big clock in the old oak tree. When the clock stops, the forest animals cannot find their way home, so children help Tikki set the hands, and the animal friends come to visit. There is a big analogue clock with hands you drag yourself, a digital clock, and five levels: whole hours, half hours, quarters, five minutes and single minutes. Children can set the clock to a given time, read the clock and choose the right time, or match a time in words, such as “quarter past three”. There is no timer and no penalty, just stars and stickers. Reading the time aloud is optional.

Made for children and designed for the Google Play Families policy: no ads, no account and no personal data collected. The whole-hour and half-hour levels are free; a one-time purchase, behind a parents-only question, unlocks the other levels and all animal stickers.
