Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Tikki Tock mobile app handles information. Tikki Tock is made for children, so it is built to collect nothing.

Effective date: 26 October 2026 · Last updated: 26 October 2026

1. Who we are

The Tikki Tock app ("the App") is developed and published by Joost Muijsenberg ("we", "us", "our"), based in the Netherlands. Contact for privacy questions: joostmuijsenberg.dev@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • The App is designed for children (about 6 to 12 years old) and their parents.
  • There are no advertisements in the App and no advertising or marketing services.
  • There is no account, no registration and no login.
  • The App does not collect, store on our servers or share any personal data. It contains no analytics and no crash-reporting service.
  • Stars, stickers, progress and settings stay on the device and are deleted when the App is uninstalled.
  • The App does not use the advertising ID, the location, the camera, the microphone, the contacts or any other personal information on the device.
  • One optional one-time purchase (the full version) is handled entirely by Google Play, behind a parents-only question in the App.

3. Information on the device

The App saves your child’s progress (stars collected, stickers, number of rounds and how often an answer was right at the first try per level) and the chosen settings (sounds, vibration, reading aloud, 12/24-hour clock, language) in the App’s own storage on the device. This data is never sent to us or to any third party. The parents page shows this progress. You can delete it at any time with “Reset progress” on the parents page, or by uninstalling the App.

4. Reading aloud

If a parent turns on “Read the time aloud”, the App asks the speech engine that is already installed on the device (text-to-speech) to read out the time. The text that is read is generated in the App from the clock time and is not sent to us. Which speech engine is used, and whether it processes text on the device or online, depends on the device’s own settings and the engine’s provider. The setting is off by default.

5. The optional purchase

The App is free to try. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks the remaining levels and the remaining animal stickers. The purchase is processed by Google Play under Google’s own terms and privacy policy; we never receive your name, email address or payment details. Google Play only tells the App whether the purchase has been made, and the App keeps that status on the device. The purchase screen is only reachable from the parents page, which is protected by a calculation that a young child cannot easily solve. There is no virtual currency, and stars and stickers cannot be bought.

6. Links

The only link in the App is this privacy policy, on the parents page. It opens in the device’s web browser, outside the App.

7. Children

The App is directed at children and follows the Google Play Families Policy. Because the App does not collect personal data from anyone, there is no personal data of children to protect, share, sell or delete on our side. The App has no social features, chat, or user-generated content.

8. Sharing of information

We do not sell, share or transfer personal information, because we do not receive any.

9. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you or your child, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. If you want to ask something anyway, or you want to lodge a complaint, contact us at joostmuijsenberg.dev@gmail.com. In the Netherlands you can also contact the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

10. Changes to this policy

If the App ever changes in a way that affects privacy (for example, if a feature that processes data is added), this policy will be updated first and the change will be announced in the Play Store listing. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest revision.
